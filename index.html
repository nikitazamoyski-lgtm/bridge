Why We Can Offer 38.5%

Physical gold is the secret engine

Retail crypto staking yields 4–8% APY. That's the open market rate. We offer 38.5% over 3 years — not by taking more risk, but by using a structural advantage: gold as collateral.

When our staking pools are backed by physically allocated gold bars in LBMA-certified vaults in Tokyo, Geneva, and Singapore, institutional lending desks offer us preferential rates. They know that even if crypto markets collapse, the gold is there.

We established direct procurement contracts with Tanaka Kikinzoku Kogyo K.K. in Hiroshima (Japan's largest gold refiner, established 1885) and the PAMP Suisse vault network — giving us institutional-grade gold at near-wholesale spreads unavailable to individual investors.

Staking rewards flow back into purchasing more gold. More gold → stronger backing → better rates → more rewards → more gold. Over 3 years, this compounding cycle generates 38.5% total return — well above what any single asset produces alone.

Our management fee is 5% of your profit only. We charge nothing upfront, nothing on your principal, and nothing if you don't earn. We win only when you win.
