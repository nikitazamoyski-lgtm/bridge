WHAT ARE LIQUID TOKENS?

When you stake, you receive liquid tokens (like stETH) that represent your staked position. These tokens:

  • Continue earning staking rewards automatically
  • Can be traded on DEXs anytime
  • Can be used as collateral in DeFi protocols
  • Can be LP tokens for additional yield
  • Are redeemable for your original tokens + rewards

WHY LIQUID STAKING MATTERS

Traditional staking locks your tokens for extended periods, preventing you from reacting to market conditions or participating in DeFi opportunities. Liquid staking solves this problem by issuing you a derivative token (like stETH or rETH) that represents your staked position. This token:

  • Continues to earn staking rewards automatically
  • Can be freely traded on decentralized exchanges
  • Can be used as collateral for loans and other DeFi protocols
  • Provides instant liquidity without waiting for unstaking periods
  • Allows you to earn "double rewards" through DeFi farming
